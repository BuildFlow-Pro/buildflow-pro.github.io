Common questions

How do I create my first estimate?

From the Home screen, tap the + button and choose Estimate. Add a customer, then add labor and material line items. You can send it for approval by email or text once it's ready.

How do payments work?

Once an estimate is approved, you can convert it into an invoice with one tap. Customers can pay by card directly from the invoice link, and BuildFlow will send automatic reminders for anything left outstanding.

Can I use BuildFlow with my crew?

Yes. You can assign jobs to crew members from the Schedule tab and they'll see their assignments and reminders.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select BuildFlow to change or cancel your plan.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at support@buildflowapp.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. Include your device model and iOS version so we can look into it faster.
