Frequently asked questions

What does BuildFlow Pro do?

It runs the paperwork side of a contracting business from your phone. You can create unlimited jobs, estimates and invoices, build invoices from line items, record payments and see what is still owed, log crew hours, attach before and after photos to a job, and see revenue and outstanding balances on a dashboard.

Where is my business data stored?

On your device. There is no account to create and no server holding your customer list, jobs, estimates or invoices. Your records live in the app's document folder, which is included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so a restored device keeps its records. Because the data is local, it does not sync between devices.

How do I create an estimate and turn it into an invoice?

Create the customer first, then create an estimate and add your labor and material line items. When the customer approves it, create an invoice from that estimate so the line items and customer details carry over. The invoice tracks what has been paid and what is still outstanding.

Can BuildFlow Pro take card payments from my customers?

No. BuildFlow Pro records payments you have already collected so your books stay accurate. It does not process card payments and it does not send payment links. You collect payment however you normally do, then record the amount and method in the app. An unpaid invoice past its due date is marked overdue automatically.

What does the subscription cost, and how do I cancel?

BuildFlow Pro Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. To manage or cancel, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then select BuildFlow. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

The app will not open, or something is broken. What do I do?

First, force quit the app and reopen it, and make sure you are on the latest version from the App Store. If it still fails, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER@example.com with your device model, your iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots help. Deleting the app removes the business records stored on that device, so contact us before you do that.

Privacy

Your jobs, estimates, invoices and customer details stay on your device. No server receives them. BuildFlow Pro does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting. The only information that leaves the device is the subscription purchase record, handled by our payment provider under an anonymous identifier. Apple processes all payments and the app never sees a card number.
