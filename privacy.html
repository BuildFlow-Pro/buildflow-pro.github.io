Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

BuildFlow Pro is built so that your business data stays on your device. This policy explains, in plain terms, what stays on your device, what leaves it, and what we can and cannot see.

What Stays On Your Device

Everything you enter into BuildFlow Pro is stored locally on your device, in the app's own document storage. This includes your business profile, your jobs, your estimates, your invoices, your time entries, your job photos, and your customers' names and contact details.

This information is never transmitted to us. We operate no server that receives it, and we cannot read it, retrieve it, or restore it. Because it lives in the app's document storage, it is included in your normal device backup, so a restored device keeps its records.

What Leaves Your Device

One thing leaves your device: your subscription purchase record. When you subscribe to BuildFlow Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple and validated by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider.

RevenueCat receives the purchase itself, which product was bought, when, and whether it is still active. It is associated with an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat generates. We do not send RevenueCat your name, your email address, or any other contact information, so the purchase record is not linked to your identity.

We use this only to unlock Pro features on your device and to see aggregate subscriber counts.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. We do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or crash reporting services. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Payment Information

All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. BuildFlow Pro never sees, receives, or stores your card number, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

Deleting Your Data

Because your business data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes it permanently. There is no account to close and no copy held elsewhere.

To cancel a subscription, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling is handled by Apple, not by us.

Children's Privacy

BuildFlow Pro is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we change what BuildFlow Pro collects, we will update this page and note the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
